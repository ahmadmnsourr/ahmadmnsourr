# Ahmad Mansour

MSc student in Mechatronics Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, specializing in hardware and embedded systems. Former Graduate Research Assistant at George Washington University.

My research focuses on **autonomous and connected vehicles**, with particular interests in motion planning, vehicle control, perception, and safety under uncertainty.

## Research Interests

- Autonomous Vehicles
- Connected Vehicles
- Intelligent Transportation Systems
- Vehicle Planning and Control
- Uncertainty-Aware Autonomous Systems

### Ongoing Research

**Planning With Imperfect Data: An OSM-Aware Adaptive-Horizon Planner for Pedestrian Safety Under Perception Degradation**

*First-Author Manuscript — In Preparation*

Research on uncertainty-aware autonomous vehicle planning, integrating map information, adaptive prediction horizons, and longitudinal control for pedestrian safety under degraded perception.
