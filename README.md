## About me

I am a mechatronics engineer pursuing an MSc in **Mechatronics Engineering** at **Politecnico di Torino**, with a focus on hardware and embedded systems. I previously worked as a **Graduate Research Assistant at George Washington University**, where my research involved autonomous and connected mobility.

My interests lie at the intersection of **autonomous vehicle motion planning, safety under uncertainty, intelligent transportation systems, robotics, and control**. My work spans research-oriented Python simulation and machine learning as well as physical systems integrating sensors, embedded controllers, mechanical actuation, and communication networks.
